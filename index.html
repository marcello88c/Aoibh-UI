THE PIPELINE

Four stages. One system. No handoff gaps.

01 / BRIEF

Structured intake

A brief goes in through a short structured form. Aoibh breaks it into tasks, formats, and deadlines automatically — no scoping calls required.

02 / DRAFT

Designers create the first draft

A matched designer from the network produces the first set of directions and variations by hand, informed by the brief and the brand's existing system.

03 / REFINE

Art direction & refinement

A senior art director selects the strongest draft and refines it — using AI for fast iteration — checking it against brand and craft standards before it moves to delivery.

04 / SHIP

Delivered, checked, and paid

Every asset is re-checked in full before it ships, then delivered directly into the brand's own systems alongside the final invoice — nothing goes out until it's been verified end to end.

QUESTIONS

Before you start a brief.

What counts as "one project"?

A single deliverable — one logo, one set of brand guidelines, one packaging concept, one landing page, and so on. Multi-part work like a full identity system is scoped and quoted separately. See the pricing section's "What counts as a project?" for the full definition.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes — subscriptions run month to month with no long-term lock-in. You can pause or cancel ahead of your next billing date; access continues through the end of the period you've already paid for.

Can I try Aoibh without subscribing?

Yes — Trial Project is a one-off way to try Aoibh on a single project, available to new clients only. Same matching, same AI + human re-check, no ongoing commitment. See pricing above.

What if I need more revisions than my plan includes?

Each plan includes a set number of revision rounds per project. If you need more, an additional round can be added to that project rather than forcing you to wait for next month.

Do unused projects roll over to the next month?

No — each plan's monthly project allowance resets at the start of your billing cycle and doesn't carry over. This keeps turnaround fast and consistent for every active client, rather than allowances stacking up unpredictably.

How fast will I get my first draft?

Two days is the standard turnaround from an approved brief to first drafts. Growth and Enterprise plans get priority turnaround on top of that.

How does designer matching actually work?

Every brief is matched against a growing global network and only assigned once the match reaches 80%+ confidence. Below that threshold, a producer steps in and matches you by hand instead of forcing a shaky fit.

Is this AI-generated work, or does a person actually design it?

Both, deliberately. A designer from the network produces the first draft by hand; an art director refines the chosen direction using AI for fast iteration; and every delivery is re-checked by both a human and AI before it ships. AI speeds up the mechanical parts of the process — a person is always responsible for the actual craft.

My project needs real functionality, not just design — can you build that too?

Subscriptions cover design. Anything requiring functionality — ecommerce integration, custom development, backend logic — sits outside subscription scope and is quoted separately. Talk to us if your project has both a design and a build side to it.

What counts as a project?

Subscriptions cover design. Anything requiring functionality — ecommerce integration, custom development, backend logic — sits outside subscription scope and is quoted separately.

How we vet every designer

  1. Portfolio & category screen. Real client work in a specific discipline — brand, motion, web, packaging — not a general portfolio.
  2. Live trial project. A real, timed brief evaluated blind by a senior art director, testing performance under real client constraints.
  3. Revision & direction test. A separate exercise testing how someone takes feedback and revises — not just raw talent, but whether they're good to work with.
  4. Reference check. Verified history with past clients or agencies.
  5. An ongoing standard, not a one-time gate. Every delivery is re-checked by an art director and AI, regardless of tenure — inclusion in the network is maintained by consistent output, not granted once and forgotten.
